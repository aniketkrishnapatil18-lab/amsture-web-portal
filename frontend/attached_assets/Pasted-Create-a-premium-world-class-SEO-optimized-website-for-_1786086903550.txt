Create a premium, world-class, SEO-optimized website for a modern IT Services and AI Solutions startup.

The website should look like a high-end consulting and technology company, designed to convert visitors into clients.

Design Style:
- Minimal, elegant, premium
- Clean white background with subtle blue gradients
- Enterprise-level UI/UX
- Modern typography
- Large whitespace
- Soft shadows
- Rounded corners
- Smooth animations
- Glassmorphism used sparingly
- Mobile-first responsive
- Fast loading
- Accessibility compliant

The design inspiration should be similar to:
- Stripe
- Linear
- Notion
- Vercel
- Framer
- Apple

Avoid:
- Generic agency templates
- Technology logos
- Developer-focused content
- Cluttered layouts
- Overly colorful designs
- Stock-looking illustrations

The website should focus on business value, trust, professionalism, and conversion.

--------------------------------------------------
WEBSITE STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------

1. Hero Section
- Powerful headline focused on business outcomes.
- Short value proposition explaining how we help businesses grow through digital solutions.
- Primary CTA: "Book a Free Consultation"
- Secondary CTA: "View Our Services"
- Premium abstract illustration or business-themed visual (no coding screens).

2. About Us
- Company story
- Vision
- Mission
- Core values
- Why clients choose us

3. Services
Present services as business solutions, including:
- Business Websites
- Custom Business Applications
- Mobile Applications
- ERP Solutions
- CRM Solutions
- AI Business Automation
- Process Automation
- Business Dashboards
- Digital Transformation
- Cloud Solutions
- Software Maintenance & Support
- Business Consulting

Each service should explain:
- Business problem
- Our solution
- Client benefits

4. Industries We Serve
- Healthcare
- Education
- Manufacturing
- Retail
- Hospitality
- Real Estate
- Finance
- Government
- Logistics
- Startups
- NGOs
- Professional Services

5. Why Choose Us
Highlight benefits instead of technologies:
- Client-first approach
- Transparent communication
- On-time delivery
- Scalable solutions
- Business-focused thinking
- Quality assurance
- Long-term partnership
- Dedicated support

6. Our Process
Visual timeline:
Discovery
↓
Requirement Analysis
↓
Planning
↓
Design
↓
Development
↓
Testing
↓
Deployment

Support & Continuous Improvement

Modern case study cards with placeholders:
- Business challenge

- Results achieved

8. Testimonials
Professional testimonial cards with ratings and client information.

9. FAQ
Answer common client questions:

10. Contact Section

11. Footer
- About
- Services
- Industries
- Portfolio
- Careers
- Blog
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Contact
- Social Media Links

SEO REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

Generate:
- SEO-friendly URLs
- Optimized page titles
- Meta descriptions
- Open Graph tags
- Twitter Card metadata
- Structured data (Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ)
- XML sitemap support
- Robots.txt
- Canonical URLs
- Breadcrumb navigation
- Semantic HTML
- Optimized heading hierarchy (H1–H3)
- Image alt text
- Internal linking strategy
- Fast Core Web Vitals performance
- Lazy loading for images
- Optimized image placeholders
